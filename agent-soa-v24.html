How each column is worked out. Commission payable: the agent's share, until it is marked Settled. Commission receivable: the full commission on policies placed through the agency, until Commission Claimed is Yes or Not Applicable (Partially Claimed still counts in full). Premium payable: only premium INSPT has received and not yet passed to the agency. Premium receivable: premium still Pending on policies the agent brought. Net is receivable less payable.
